1. What is Odoo?
Odoo is an open-source business management software that provides different applications to manage and automate business operations.

It is an ERP platform developed using Python and PostgreSQL.

For example, suppose a company wants to manage:
- Employees and their attendance.
- Sales and customer details.
- Products and inventory.
- Accounting and invoices.
- Purchase orders.
Instead of using different software for everything, the company can use Odoo to manage all these operations from one integrated platform.
